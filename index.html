About Ús

For as long as there have been museums, there have been juries, and for as long as there have been juries there has been a staircase going down. L'Oubliette is at the bottom of it.

We keep what the Salon sent home, what the Church refused to hang, what thieves cut from its frame and what painters covered with something more sellable. Every work is real. Every refusal happened.

We don't restore. We don't apologise. We remember, which is more than anyone else did.

refusée ✦ guarded

Scandal, n. what happens when a painting looks back. In 1863 the jury sent her home. In 1865 they hung her, and had to post two guards beside her, because Paris kept trying to hit her with umbrellas.

“She is not a goddess. She is not a nymph. She is a woman in a room, and she knows exactly who just walked in.” — the keepers

— having caused a public outrage, twice —

Every refusal in this building started the same way: someone looked for less than a second, felt something they didn't order, and blamed the painting.
