Leonardo

paints the Battle of Anghiari on the council hall wall, trying an oil technique of his own. The colours run. He leaves for Milan with it half done.

Vasari

remodels the hall for the Medici and covers the walls with his own battles. He admired Leonardo. On a green flag he paints two tiny words.

04

Trova

TROVA [it.] — (he, she) finds. painted on a flag, 12 m up, by Vasari.

In 2011 a team drilled a few small holes through cracks in Vasari's fresco and slid a camera behind it. They found a gap, and on the far wall a black pigment close to the one Leonardo used on the Mona Lisa.

Then the drilling was stopped: you don't make holes in a Vasari to look for a Leonardo. The probe came out. Both are still there, one in front of the other.

Rage

Be afraid of a patient woman
with a meat cleaver up her sleeve.

On 10 March 1914 Mary Richardson walked into the National Gallery and cut the Rokeby Venus seven times. She said she had damaged the most beautiful woman in mythology because the government was destroying Mrs Pankhurst, the most beautiful character in modern history. Years later she added that she hadn't liked the way men gaped at it all day. The cuts were mended within months. They are still there, under the retouching.
