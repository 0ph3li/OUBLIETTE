Descénte

Piranesi drew these prisons around 1750, out of nothing, and every one of them is mostly stairs. Ours are fewer and less dramatic. Sixty-three steps, one landing, one door, and three dogs at the bottom who will want to know why you came.

  1. Find the Cour Carrée. The square courtyard at the old end of the Louvre, the one without a pyramid. If you can see the pyramid, turn around.
  2. Find the door marked “staff only”. There are eleven. Ours is the one nobody is guarding.
  3. Go down. The light goes first. After the twentieth step it is candles, and after the fortieth it is mostly the candle you brought.
  4. Stop at the landing. There is a chair. It was refused by the Salon of 1863. Do not sit on it.
  5. Say nothing. Sound carries down here and the hidden wing is trying to sleep.
  6. Meet the keepers. One of them will lean on you. This is the ticket check.

There is no lift. There was one in 1989. It was refused.
