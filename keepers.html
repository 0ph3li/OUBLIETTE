Gardiens

Three borzois keep the keys of the oubliette. They walk the stairs at night, fourteen metres of them, and they sniff every visitor on the way back up. Whatever you saw down here, they make sure you forget it, and they are very good at their job. They were not hired. They came down one winter and refused to leave, which, in this museum, is the only qualification there is.

From ten at night to six in the morning one borzoi watches and one sleeps, and they swap every hour on the hour. Nobody taught them this. Nobody has ever checked whether the one on watch is awake.
